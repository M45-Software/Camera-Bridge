# Camera Bridge 0.3.1

- Add a separate **Camera controls…** window, accessible from the tray and main
  window, with Exposure, Color, and Focus tabs.
- Offer camera-supported ISO, shutter speed, aperture, exposure compensation,
  white balance, Kelvin temperature, Picture Style, and live-view AF method
  choices. Settings locked by the camera mode or lens remain disabled.
- Add one-shot autofocus and fine, medium, or coarse near/far focus buttons.
  Controls share the active Canon SDK session, so changes can be made during
  video without opening a competing USB connection.
- Read idle camera settings without enabling live view. Camera changes apply
  immediately and are not reset when the controls window opens or closes.
- Show **Reconnecting…** in the tray and main window when the camera disappears
  or live-view frames stop. Confirm capture only after a complete camera frame
  arrives, and return to live status when frames resume.
- Include camera-control screenshots and explain the EOS 7D Mark II autofocus
  limitations. Its Movie Servo AF and Continuous AF settings are not exposed by
  the tested SDK; configure Movie Servo AF on the camera. Canon disables Movie
  Servo AF at 59.94/50 fps on this body; use 29.97/30 fps for continuous autofocus.

Live controls require the Canon SDK backend. gphoto2 remains available for
capture fallback but cannot share its active USB session with these controls.

Validated on an EOS 7D Mark II: exposure, color, and AF-method changes read back
correctly while camera frames continued; one-shot AF and near/far lens commands
succeeded. Test exposure/color settings were restored afterward. Automated
validation includes 48 tests, Qt control-window checks, shell validation, and
Debian package construction.
